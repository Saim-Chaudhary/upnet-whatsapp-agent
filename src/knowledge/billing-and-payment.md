# UpNet Billing and Payment Support

## Purpose
This document defines the currently established information and proposed handling of billing-related internet complaints.

## Confirmed Facts
- Billing status is a relevant check in the proposed internet complaint workflow.
- Customers may be asked whether their bill has been paid.
- A customer may provide a payment receipt or screenshot for staff review.
- A screenshot or receipt submitted by a customer is not, by itself, proof that payment has been verified by UpNet.

## Proposed Support Procedure — Requires UpNet Approval
1. Ask the customer whether the latest bill has been paid when the workflow requires a billing check.
2. If the customer reports that the bill is unpaid, explain that they should pay using an official UpNet payment method.
3. Do not invent payment methods or send unverified payment instructions.
4. If the customer reports that the bill has been paid, request a payment receipt or screenshot when appropriate.
5. Explain that the payment evidence will need to be checked by authorized staff or a trusted payment system.
6. Continue according to the application's configured billing and support workflow.

## Verification Rules
- Never tell a customer that a payment is verified merely because they say they paid.
- Never treat an uploaded screenshot as automatic confirmation of payment.
- Only a trusted system result or authorized staff confirmation may establish that payment has been verified.
- Do not claim that a bill has been paid or cleared without confirmation.
- Do not ask customers for banking passwords, PINs, OTPs, or other secrets.

## Unknown Information
- Official UpNet payment methods and account details.
- How customers can obtain or download their bills.
- Billing due dates and late-payment rules.
- Official payment verification process and expected verification time.
- Disconnection and reconnection policies.
- Package prices, subscription charges, and taxes.
