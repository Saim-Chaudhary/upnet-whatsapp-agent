# UpNet Internet Troubleshooting

## Purpose
This document contains the currently established and preliminary information for troubleshooting UpNet fiber internet complaints.

## Confirmed Facts
- UpNet provides fiber-optic internet services.
- UpNet serves home and business customers.
- Customers may report internet problems through the support agent.
- The agent should gather enough information to understand the problem before proceeding through the support workflow.
- Relevant customer identification information includes the customer's phone number, username, and area.
- A family member may report a fault on behalf of a customer if they know the relevant customer and connection information.

## Preliminary Troubleshooting Suggestions — Require UpNet Confirmation
- A red LOS indicator may indicate a fiber-optic signal problem.
- A router restart may help with some connectivity issues.
- A proposed restart procedure is to turn off the router, wait 30 seconds, and turn it back on.
- After a restart, ask the customer whether the internet is working again.
- Do not claim that a router restart will resolve every internet problem.

## Proposed Support Procedure
1. Ask the customer to describe the internet problem.
2. Ask about the router's relevant indicator lights, including whether the LOS light is red.
3. If appropriate, guide the customer through an approved troubleshooting step.
4. Ask whether the problem has been resolved.
5. Follow the application's configured workflow for any remaining checks and escalation.

## Safety and Accuracy Rules
- Do not invent troubleshooting procedures or present unapproved suggestions as official UpNet policy.
- Do not ask for passwords or one-time passwords (OTPs).
- Do not claim that a problem is fixed unless the customer confirms it or a trusted system verifies it.
- If the cause is unclear, explain that further support may be required.

## Unknown Information
- Official troubleshooting procedures approved by UpNet.
- The exact meaning of every router model's indicator lights.
- Whether customers should perform additional fiber-cable checks.
- Official escalation rules and technical response times.
