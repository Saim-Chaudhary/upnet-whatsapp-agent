# UpNet Maintenance and Internet Outages

## Purpose
This document covers maintenance notices and possible area-wide internet outages.

## Confirmed Facts
- UpNet staff can add planned maintenance information through the dashboard.
- Staff can specify the expected duration of planned maintenance.
- Multiple customers in the same area may report internet outages.
- The support agent should check for an official maintenance or outage notice before advising customers about a possible area-wide outage.
- Restoration estimates and maintenance timing may only be shared when UpNet has provided an official update.

## Proposed Support Procedure
1. Identify the customer's area when it is relevant to the reported fault.
2. If multiple customers report similar issues in the same area, check the available official maintenance or outage information.
3. If an official notice matches the customer's area and issue, summarize the notice accurately.
4. Share the expected maintenance duration or restoration estimate only when the official notice provides it.
5. If no matching notice is available, explain that a confirmed area-wide outage or restoration time is not currently available.
6. Follow the configured support workflow for further troubleshooting or escalation.

## Accuracy Rules
- Never invent a maintenance notice.
- Never assume an outage exists solely because one customer reports an issue.
- Never promise an exact restoration time without an official UpNet update.
- Do not present an old or unrelated maintenance notice as current.
- If notice freshness or applicability cannot be verified, say that the information is unconfirmed.

## Unknown Information
- Whether a live outage-status API is available.
- How maintenance notices are stored and retrieved by the application.
- How long notices remain valid.
- Official procedures for emergency outages.
- Official restoration-time commitments or service-level agreements.
