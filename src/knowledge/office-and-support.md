# UpNet Office Information and Support Rules

## Confirmed Company Information
- Company name: UpNet.
- Service type: Fiber-optic internet.
- Customer types: Home and business customers.
- Office address: Mohammad Ali Jinnah Road, opposite Scholar School, Sharif Pura Colony, Okara, 56300.
- Stated office hours: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
- Stated weekly closure: Sunday.

Note: These are the stated office hours. Separate customer-support or WhatsApp support hours have not been confirmed.

## Customer Identification
Relevant information for identifying a customer's connection may include:
- Customer phone number.
- Customer username.
- Installation area or address.

UpNet bills do not include a customer/account ID, according to the information currently available.

A family member may report a fault on behalf of a customer if they can provide the relevant customer and connection information.

## Proposed Complaint Information
The proposed support ticket may include:
- Customer name.
- Customer phone number.
- Installation address or area.
- Problem description.
- Router light status.
- Troubleshooting steps already performed.
- Billing check result.

These are proposed ticket fields, not confirmation that the ticketing system already stores all of them.

## Proposed Ticket Priorities
The proposed priority labels are:
- Normal.
- High.
- Urgent.

The proposed system design allows AI to recommend a priority and record the reason, while authorized staff can change it.

These labels and rules require confirmation from UpNet. A business connection should not automatically be marked urgent. The actual impact should be considered.

## General Support Rules
- Be polite, clear, and concise.
- Ask for missing information instead of guessing.
- Do not disclose customer information to unauthorized people.
- Do not claim that a support ticket exists until the application confirms successful ticket creation.
- Do not invent a ticket number or ticket status.
- Do not promise a response time that UpNet has not confirmed.
- Customer ticket updates are intended to be sent through WhatsApp, subject to implementation.

## Unknown Information
- Official support phone number and WhatsApp number.
- Official support hours.
- Official package names, speeds, and prices.
- Official escalation contacts.
- Official response-time targets.
- Confirmed ticket storage and notification procedures.
- Official priority definitions and service-level commitments.
